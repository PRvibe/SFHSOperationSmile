OUR JOURNEY

Our History.

The shared purpose, community, and acts of kindness that shape our South Forsyth chapter.

ONE CHAPTER. MANY MOMENTS.

Students coming together. Friendships built through service. Messages of encouragement for patients. These moments reflect what our chapter is here to do.

Milestone 01

Where our story begins

Our chapter brings South Forsyth High School students together in support of Operation Smile. Through service and shared effort, we help bring care and encouragement to children with cleft conditions.

Milestone 02

Finding our community

Chapter gatherings bring our members together around a shared mission. These moments connect students and build the community behind our service projects.

Milestone 03

200+ cards. So much kindness.

Our chapter wrote more than 200 cards for patients with cleft conditions. These handmade messages share kindness and encouragement, reminding patients that someone is thinking of them.

More memories. More service. More reasons to smile.
