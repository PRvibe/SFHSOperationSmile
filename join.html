FIND YOUR WAY TO HELP

There’s more than
one way to
create a smile.

Whether you love organizing, creating, or simply showing up for others, there’s a place for you in our chapter.

01

Volunteer

Bring your time and energy to chapter service projects. Help with planning, lend a hand at an event, or team up with other members to make an idea happen.

02

Attend meetings

Meet the team, hear what’s coming up, and share your ideas. Find current meeting information and chapter updates through our Linktree.

03

Support drives

Help collect, organize, and sort items for chapter drives. Check the current collection list with our officers before bringing items, so every contribution meets the project’s needs.

04

Create patient cards

Put your creativity and kindness on paper. Join a card-making activity and follow the chapter’s guidance to create thoughtful, encouraging messages for patients with cleft conditions.

05

Help with fundraisers

Make the next chapter fundraiser a team effort. Contribute ideas, help organize supplies, promote the event, or volunteer on the day.

06

Spread awareness

Learn about Operation Smile’s mission, share reliable information about cleft conditions, and invite classmates to get involved. A conversation can be someone’s first step toward helping.

START SMALL. START HERE.

Your first step
is a simple one.

  1. Connect with our chapter

    Open our Linktree to find membership information and the latest chapter links.

  2. Find out what’s next

    Look for meeting announcements and current opportunities. Our officers can help you find your place.

  3. Show up. Make a difference.

    Meet fellow members, choose an activity, and put your compassion into action.
